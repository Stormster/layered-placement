[h1]Layered Placement[/h1]
Use normal [b]Move Furniture → Place[/b] for lights, posters, and other wall/ceiling decor — closer to what the admin brush can do, without full Place Anywhere.

[h2]What you can do[/h2]
[list]
[*] Put ceiling lights and string lights over tables, counters, posters, and other clutter
[*] Stack more than one high decor item on the same tile
[*] Keep wall decorations together (for example a light and a poster on the same wall)
[*] Place hanging decor and outdoor/wall lamps on railings, posts, and catwalk edges
[*] Turn railing lamps on and off more reliably (right-click / left-click without the rail stealing every click)
[*] When you're upstairs on mesh floors and the cursor drops to the ground, Place aims at your floor instead
[*] Finish Place and Pickup when you're already next to awkward catwalk tiles but pathing fails
[/list]

[h2]What it does [b]not[/b] do[/h2]
[list]
[*] Not full Place Anywhere
[*] Crates, tables, chairs, and other floor furniture still follow vanilla stacking
[*] You still use the item from inventory and still need the usual skills / tools
[/list]

[h2]Options[/h2]
[b]Mod Options → Layered Placement[/b] — separate toggles (all on by default):
[list]
[*] Lights over furniture & stacked decor
[*] Place on railings & catwalks
[*] Aim at your floor through mesh
[*] Easier reach on catwalks
[*] Easier railing light controls
[/list]

With Mod Manager, use the [b]Mod Options[/b] screen (Esc → Options → Mods is often empty).

[h2]Multiplayer[/h2]
[b]Very work in progress.[/b] Install on the [b]server and all clients[/b], but expect breakage.

Some features may not work at all in MP yet (especially mesh-floor aiming, catwalk reach, and railing light controls). Placement helpers depend on the server having the mod loaded; client-only features follow each player’s Mod Options and may desync or fail on dedicated servers.

[h2]Requirements[/h2]
Build 42.20+. Safe to add mid-save.

[h2]Feedback[/h2]
If something looks wrong or won’t toggle, note the item name and where you placed it.
